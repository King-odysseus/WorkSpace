# Screen-sharing and screenshot policy

WorkSpace screen sharing is an optional, visible support and supervision tool. It must be used only for a stated, legitimate work purpose and in accordance with employment, privacy, monitoring, and consultation requirements that apply to the company and employee.

A manager may send a request, but cannot start sharing. The employee must explicitly accept and select a screen, window, or browser tab in the browser’s own sharing picker. The browser shows its normal sharing indicator, and WorkSpace continuously displays **Screen sharing active** with a stop button. The employee may decline or stop at any time without the application attempting to restart capture.

WorkSpace takes still screenshots only during that active, consented session and at the interval shown to the employee. It does not request or collect audio or webcam data and does not provide remote control. Employees should share the smallest suitable window or tab and close sensitive personal or third-party information before accepting.

Only authorised workspace owners and managers may access other people's screenshots, and only they may delete them. Employees can review and download the screenshots taken of their own sessions. Listing, viewing, downloading, deleting, and automatic expiry are logged. Downloads must remain within approved company systems and must not be shared beyond people with a legitimate need. Screenshots are automatically deleted after the configured short retention period, currently shown in the Screen sharing page. A manager may delete them earlier.

Employees should contact their manager or privacy contact with questions or to exercise applicable data rights. Enabling this feature does not replace legal review, employee consultation, a records-retention assessment, or any notice/consent required in the jurisdictions where the company operates.
